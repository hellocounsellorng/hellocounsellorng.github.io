ABOUT HELLO COUNSELLOR NG

A place to talk, listen, and connect.

Hello Counsellor NG is a peer-support community created for people who want a safe place to share what they are going through.

Life can be complicated. Sometimes you need advice. Sometimes you need a different perspective. Sometimes you simply need to say something out loud without being judged.

Our community brings people together to share experiences, offer thoughtful advice, listen with empathy, and remind one another that no one has to face everything alone.

Your story does not have to be perfect, polished, or easy to explain. You can simply tell it as it is.

What happens here?

Members can submit their stories, questions, situations, experiences, or thoughts anonymously.

The Hello Counsellor NG team reviews submissions before deciding which ones are appropriate to share with the community.

Once shared, members can discuss the situation, offer perspectives, and support the person behind the story.

PRIVACY MATTERS

Protect your identity.

You can share your story anonymously, but please be careful not to include information that could identify you or someone else.

What should I leave out?

You can submit your message anonymously. Please do not include your real name, phone number, address, social media handles, photographs, or any other information that could identify you or another person.

If you need to mention someone's name, please use a fake name that does not correspond to the person's real identity.

You are also welcome to include your age and gender, as well as the age and gender of other people involved, if relevant.

For example:
"I am 28, female."
"The person I'm talking about is 45, male."
A note from the HC team: Your submission will be reviewed by the Hello Counsellor NG team and, where appropriate, may be shared anonymously in our community for discussion, advice, and support. Not every submission will necessarily be posted. Moderators may choose not to publish harmful, abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, identifying, or otherwise inappropriate content.
COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Keep this space safe for everyone.

Hello Counsellor NG is built around empathy, respect, confidentiality, and constructive conversation.

1

Be respectful.

No insults, mockery, shaming, or harsh language toward anyone or toward anonymous submissions.

2

No victim-blaming or harassment.

Everyone's experience deserves empathy, even when you disagree with their choices or perspective.

3

No hate speech.

Discrimination or hateful comments based on gender, religion, tribe, sexuality, background, or other personal characteristics are not allowed.

4

Give constructive advice only.

Do not encourage self-harm, violence, revenge, dangerous behaviour, or illegal actions.

5

No sexual or explicit comments.

Do not sexualize anonymous submissions or turn someone's vulnerable experience into sexual discussion.

6

Confidentiality is mandatory.

Do not screenshot, forward, share, or attempt to identify the person behind an anonymous submission.

7

Moderators' decisions are final.

Members may receive up to three warnings for breaking community rules. Continued disregard after the third warning may result in removal from the community without further notice.
