Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before sharing something with us.

Is this actually anonymous?

Yes. We never ask for your name, and your submission isn't linked to any personal account. If you choose to leave contact info (like when requesting a counselor), that's entirely optional and only seen by our team.

What happens after I submit my story?

Our team reviews it, then shares it with the community exactly as you wrote it. We don't edit your words — the voice you told it in is yours, and it stays yours.

Will my submission definitely be posted?

Yes — we always publish. HC isn't a place where your voice gets filtered out; every submission becomes part of the community.

Can people reply or comment on my story?

Yes — once your story is posted on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or X, people can comment to offer advice, support, or their own perspective.

Where do submissions get posted?

Across our WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and X communities. We spread submissions across all four so every platform has fresh stories, rather than posting every story everywhere.

How do I talk to a real counselor instead of posting publicly?

Visit our Counselors page — you can reach out directly via WhatsApp, call, or email, completely separate from the anonymous story process.

Is there someone to talk to if I'm in crisis right now?

Right now, you can reach one of our counselors directly via the Counselors page — WhatsApp, call, or email. We're building toward a dedicated toll-free crisis line so no one is ever held back by not having airtime. Until then, our counselors are real people who choose to be reachable, not a hotline queue.

If you're in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, please contact your nearest hospital or emergency service as well.
Do you store my information?

We only store what you choose to submit. We don't collect names, emails, or identifying details unless you provide them yourself (for example, when requesting a counselor).

Can I request my story be taken down after posting?

Yes. Anonymity doesn't end once a story is posted — if you change your mind, you can request a takedown at any time, no questions asked.
